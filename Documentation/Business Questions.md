# Business Questions

## Overview

The objective of this analysis is to understand customer purchasing behavior, product performance, reorder patterns, and shopping trends using the Instacart dataset. The following business questions will guide the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard development.

---

# 1. Executive KPIs

The project will answer the following high-level business questions:

* How many total orders were placed?
* How many unique customers are in the dataset?
* How many unique products are available?
* How many departments and aisles exist?
* What is the overall reorder rate?
* What is the average basket size?

---

# 2. Customer Analysis

The following questions help understand customer purchasing behavior:

* How many orders has each customer placed?
* Who are the most active customers?
* What is the average number of orders per customer?
* How many days do customers typically wait before placing another order?
* Which customers have the highest reorder rates?

---

# 3. Product Analysis

These questions evaluate product performance:

* Which products are ordered most frequently?
* Which products are ordered least frequently?
* Which products have the highest reorder rates?
* Which products are rarely reordered?
* What are the Top 20 best-selling products?

---

# 4. Department Analysis

These questions evaluate department performance:

* Which departments generate the highest number of orders?
* Which departments contain the most products?
* Which departments have the highest reorder rates?
* Which departments contribute the largest share of total sales volume?

---

# 5. Aisle Analysis

These questions analyze product categories at the aisle level:

* Which aisles receive the highest number of orders?
* Which aisles contain the largest number of products?
* Which aisles have the highest reorder rates?

---

# 6. Order Trend Analysis

These questions identify customer shopping patterns over time:

* On which day of the week are the most orders placed?
* During which hours of the day do customers shop most frequently?
* What are the peak shopping periods?
* Are there noticeable shopping patterns throughout the week?

---

# 7. Basket Analysis

These questions examine customer purchasing habits within individual orders:

* What is the average basket size?
* What is the largest basket recorded?
* Which products are most frequently purchased together?
* Which products are commonly the first items added to the cart?

---

# 8. Reorder Analysis

These questions measure customer loyalty and repeat purchasing behavior:

* What percentage of purchased products are reordered?
* Which products have the highest reorder frequency?
* Which departments generate the most repeat purchases?
* Which aisles have the strongest customer loyalty?

---

# 9. Advanced Business Analysis

The following analyses provide deeper business insights:

* Rank the best-selling products within each department.
* Identify the most popular product in every department.
* Segment customers based on their purchasing frequency.
* Identify customers with consistently high purchase activity.
* Analyze the relationship between basket size and reorder behavior.
* Identify products that are frequently purchased together for cross-selling opportunities.

---

# Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis is expected to provide insights that can help Instacart:

* Improve inventory planning for high-demand products.
* Optimize product recommendations based on purchasing patterns.
* Increase customer retention through reorder analysis.
* Identify top-performing departments and aisles.
* Support cross-selling and promotional strategies using market basket analysis.
* Understand customer shopping behavior by day and time.
* Enable data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.
